GapFill for Krita

Choose the transparent Coloring layer, transparent-background Line Art layer, and optional Guides layer, then select Scan / Activate.

Hover a circle for a fixed 5× magnifier. Drag from inside a circle to sample a corrected color. Drag from outside the circles to sweep over suggestions, or use Apply All.

The white Background layer is intentionally not selected: only the Coloring layer's transparency defines unpainted pixels. Line Art and Guides also need transparent backgrounds because their alpha channels define boundaries.
