GapFill for Krita

Choose the transparent Coloring layer, transparent-background Line Art layer, and optional Guides layer, then select Scan / Activate.

Hover a circle for a fixed 5× magnifier. Drag from inside a circle to sample a corrected color. Drag from outside the circles to sweep over suggestions, or use Apply All.

The white Background layer is intentionally not selected: only the Coloring layer's transparency defines unpainted pixels. Line Art and Guides also need transparent backgrounds because their alpha channels define boundaries.

Safe Coloring targets are currently origin-aligned, unlocked, RGBA/U8 Paint Layers without masks, effects, animation, styles, opacity, non-Normal blending, inherit alpha, or non-neutral parents. Mixed-profile and larger-than-4096²-equivalent documents are rejected.

Rescan after any layer, document, selection, or active-node change. A successful Apply also clears every remaining suggestion and requires a rescan.

The interactive overlay is disabled for rotation, mirroring, unqualified HiDPI, or an ambiguous split-view canvas. Apply restores the exact selection presence, foreground, active node, and relevant view/tool state and verifies target pixels. Public LibKis cannot group its internal selection actions into one Undo step, so atomic one-step Undo is not promised.

Real Krita host qualification is still incomplete; the offscreen Qt and fake-adapter tests are not host certification.
