RECOVERED OFFF DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION STATUS
======================================================================

The historical specification describes experimental Overflow Flood Fill for
clean binary line art. It anticipates small transparent Coloring gaps during
bucket fill, builds large owner regions separated by Line Art and Guides,
precomputes a gap-to-owner assignment from ONNX probabilities, previews linked
gaps on hover, and propagates clicked/current color on click. The output is used
for owner selection, not direct color prediction. It specifies one history
operation, standard bucket fallback outside an owner, and one immediate retry
suppression after undo.

Current code is a web-only prototype, not a Krita OFFF implementation.

SPECIFIED:

- problem and interaction model;
- small-gap and Guide-associated gap concepts;
- owner-region and mean-likelihood assignment concepts;
- independent bucket mode, hover, click propagation, fallback, undo retry;
- binary-input/four-connected limitations and planned anti-alias work.

IMPLEMENTED on the historical web branch:

- web/src/overflow/ownerRegions.ts: four-neighbor owner components;
- web/src/overflow/precompute.ts: target mask, ONNX probability map, mean owner
  score in the patch, abort/progress checks;
- web/src/overflow/paint.ts: clicked/current color propagation;
- web/src/overflow/types.ts and rendering/control components;
- web/src/overflow/useOverflowFill.ts and central web UI integration;
